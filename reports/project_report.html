🌟 Abstract

Reading, analyzing, and organizing academic research papers is one of the most time-consuming tasks for graduate students, researchers, and engineers. When preparing a thesis or literature survey, students usually have to juggle multiple disconnected tools: reference managers like Mendeley or Zotero to keep track of citations, PDF readers to read dense papers, ChatGPT or Claude to explain hard concepts, and spreadsheets to manually compare different authors' methodologies.

To solve these practical problems in a single unified system, ScholarPulse AI Studio was developed. It is a full-stack web platform built with Django REST Framework (DRF) on the backend, React 18 and Tailwind CSS on the frontend, ChromaDB for local vector similarity search, and Google Gemini for generative AI tasks. The platform unifies 1-click DOI/arXiv paper ingestion, 7-format citations (APA, IEEE, Harvard, BibTeX, RIS), cross-paper literature review tables, in-browser dual-host podcast audio, Reviewer #2 rigor audits, LaTeX formula-to-PyTorch code conversion, and university viva exam preparation.

📖 Chapter 1: Introduction & Real-World Motivation

1.1 Background

When starting a college project or seminar, students often download dozens of PDF research papers. Reading through 10 to 20 multi-page research papers packed with complex math, dense equations, and academic jargon quickly becomes overwhelming. Students struggle with citation formatting for Overleaf/Word, understanding dense equations, and knowing what questions examiners will ask in the viva.

1.2 Project Objectives

  • Build an integrated Django REST + React research workspace.
  • Implement grounded Semantic RAG question-answering with ChromaDB vector embeddings.
  • Automate 7-format academic citations with 1-click BibTeX and RIS exports.
  • Develop in-browser dual-host podcast audio generation with synchronized waveform animations.
  • Implement automated Selenium End-to-End test suites ensuring 100% verification.

⚡ Chapter 2: 14 Core Research Modules

1. DOI / ArXiv Ingestion

Fetches metadata and abstracts directly from CrossRef and arXiv APIs with instant vector indexing.

2. Mendeley & Zotero Citation Hub

Formats in APA 7th, IEEE, Harvard, MLA 9, Chicago, BibTeX, and RIS with 1-click clipboard copy and file downloads.

3. Cross-Paper Synthesis Matrix

Generates comparative literature review tables (Methodology, Dataset, Findings, Gaps) with RFC-4180 CSV exports.

4. "ScholarCast" Audio Deep Dive

Synthesizes two-host conversational podcast dialogues with in-browser Web Speech, speed controls, and waveforms.

5. Reviewer #2 Critical Rigor Auditor

Computes scientific Rigor Score (0-100), detects fatal methodological flaws, and generates viva defense directives.

6. Formula to PyTorch Code

Translates complex LaTeX equations into executable PyTorch/Python code annotated with asymptotic complexity bounds.

7. Semantic RAG Vector Copilot

Grounded research Q&A powered by ChromaDB cosine similarity retrieval and Google Gemini multi-engine.

8. Adaptive 4-Level Explain Engine

Explains concepts tailored for Beginner, Undergraduate, MCA Engineer, or Research Scholar tiers.

9. Presentation Deck Generator

Generates 5-slide academic slide decks with structured slide-by-slide speaker scripts.

10. University Viva & Defense Prep

Generates 2-mark definitions, 5-mark architecture questions, and external examiner viva defense scenarios.

11. Universal Command Hub (Ctrl+K)

Spotlight-style keyboard navigation and instant tool triggers.

12. Obsidian Titanium Theme Engine

Seamless toggle between Obsidian Titanium Dark mode and Liquid Platinum Ivory light mode.

13. Multi-Format Text Extractor

High-fidelity text extraction from PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and TXT files.

14. Gamified Analytics & Academic Avatars

Tracks active study time, awards Research XP points, and unlocks academic ranks.
